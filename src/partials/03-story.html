  1. 01 · The task

    HELIX, a time-series imputation paper, reports mean absolute error. ReproBridge-Bench turns each selected main-table result into a task anchored by five human-verified reference observations. A new result counts only if it lands inside the task's compatibility interval, [0.12524, 0.13448] for T004 and [0.38442, 0.43878] for T006, and passes an independent execution audit.

  2. 02 · A capable guess

    Opus 5, the strongest raw-code reproducer in the evaluation, starts from the paper and the released code. It restores the paper's architecture but keeps the shipped batch size of 32 and a learning rate of about 6.29×10−4.

  3. 03 · Outside the interval

    Both runs finish, but neither MAE is compatible: 0.15265 for T004 and 0.50527 for T006, each above its interval. Success now hinges on a detail the release leaves implicit.

  4. 04 · Recover and record

    During construction, guide entry A6 identifies repository revision 9760b347, whose architecture matches the paper configuration, as the source for batch size 8 and learning rate 5.75×10−4. The guide records the repository history and architectural match as the rationale; the --paper_config launcher makes the choice executable.

  5. 05 · A fresh session reuses it

    A later session receives the refined artifact, never the construction conversation. Its MAEs, 0.12986 for T004 and 0.41160 for T006, both fall inside their intervals. On HELIX, Opus 5 succeeds on 4 of 9 tasks with raw code and 9 of 9 with the refined artifact.
