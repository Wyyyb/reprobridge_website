06 · ScopeIntended use, and what the results do not show

Reproduction success depends jointly on artifact quality and reproducer ability. ReproBridge is primarily intended as a reproducibility check before a paper is submitted and its artifacts are released, and the same format lets third-party reproducers preserve their work to lower the cost of later attempts.

Primary use · Authors

Before submission

The primary intended use is for authors to apply ReproBridge as a reproducibility check before submitting a paper and releasing its artifacts. Executing the paper's specified setup can expose missing details, execution barriers, and paper–code inconsistencies, giving authors concrete issues to inspect and correct.

Paper–code misalignment

When the paper and released code conflict, ReproBridge strictly follows the paper's setup and flags the discrepancy with source locations and affected tasks. Such records help authors check consistency between their manuscript and implementation before submission.

What these results do not show

The limitations stated in the paper's discussion, each linked to the part of this page it qualifies.

01

Conditional evaluation

The evaluation conditions on retained papers, fixed reference regions, and the tested agents; it is not a conference-wide reproduction rate. Five reference observations are a protocol choice, not a guarantee of adequate uncertainty estimation.

The 36 papers were retained from a random sample of 50 of the 241 ICML 2026 Spotlights with code and non-purely-theoretical content.

Related section: 02 · Benchmark ↑
02

Compatibility is not validity

Numerical compatibility does not certify scientific validity or complete fidelity to the stated experiment, and refined artifacts can preserve incorrect assumptions.

Success requires numerical compatibility and no confirmed audit violation.

Related section: 02 · Benchmark ↑
03

Imperfect audits

Automated audits miss some violations, as quantified against human verification.

Of the 39 invalid outcomes identified in human review of 818 outcomes, Codex misses eight and Claude misses 15.

Related section: 02 · Benchmark ↑
04

Workflow and handoff together

Ordinary outputs and refined artifacts come from separate runs with comparable upstream resource use, so the comparison evaluates the construction workflow and resulting handoff together; it does not isolate the effect of reorganizing identical outputs. Component ablations measure losses conditional on the remaining materials.

Related section: 03 · Results ↑
05

Descriptive annotations

Case annotations and variability strata describe patterns rather than isolate causal effects.

Explanations for recovered outcomes come from an agent-assisted case review of 101 model–paper pairs; their counts are not independent causal measurements.

Related section: 05 · Cases ↑
06

Partial cost accounting

API savings concern downstream Reproducer sessions. Construction, independent verification, GPU execution, maintenance, and human review have separate costs; reuse estimates assume fixed prices and savings. Human time savings and comprehension require a separate study.
