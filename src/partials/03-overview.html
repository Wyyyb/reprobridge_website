Overview

Preserve what a reproduction recovers, then measure whether it helps

ReproBridge has three parts. A standardized refined reproducible artifact preserves executable procedures and the reasons behind them. An agent workflow constructs these artifacts from a paper and its official repository. ReproBridge-Bench measures whether fresh coding-agent sessions reproduce experiments more reliably with them.

01Artifact

Refined reproducible artifact

Four components: a refined codebase, environment setup, experiment launchers, and a reproduction guide. Together they preserve executable procedures and the reasons for their settings and repairs.
