03 · ResultsMore reproductions succeed, at lower downstream cost

Fresh Reproducer sessions receive the paper, the selected tasks, and either the raw codebase or its refined artifact. Across four models, refined artifacts raise mean success from 76.4% to 92.6%, narrow the cross-model spread from 21.6 to 3.9 pp, and reduce downstream API spending by 19.7% on average.

Each component contributes to reuse

Ordinary outputs are Opus 5's raw-code execution directory with code, scripts, and logs retained and result files removed; they carry no standardized guide. The complete artifact outperforms ordinary outputs by 11.8 pp, and removing any component lowers success.

Reuse can offset construction API cost

Constructing the 36 artifacts costs $5,026 in API usage ($139.61 per paper). At the mean downstream costs, break-even requires 8, 13, and 9 reuse rounds for Sonnet 4.6, Sonnet 5, and Opus 5, saving $653, $409, and $615 per complete round.

Cumulative API spending (construction + Reproducer)

Raw reuse costs n times the mean raw-code round; refined reuse adds the $5,026 construction cost once, then n times the mean refined-artifact round. The paper reports this scenario for Sonnet 4.6, Sonnet 5, and Opus 5.
