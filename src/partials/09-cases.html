The released TinyImageNet loader uses ImageFolder on a validation directory containing only an images subdirectory. It therefore assigns all 10,000 validation images to one class. From the paper · Appendix, failure modes and concentrated regressionsAuxiliaryMCMC: numerical fragility interacts with a finite budget.

The Opus 5 refined-artifact Gaussian sweep for AuxiliaryMCMC targets 100 rounds at each of three acceptance rates. Its measured workload exceeds the available clock at the chosen concurrency, and the agent executes only 55 rounds per rate. The primary audit invalidates numerical matches affected by this altered measurement procedure. Separately, one Poisson-MALA chain remains constant with zero acceptance. Its undefined effective sample size propagates through the evaluator's ordinary mean, leaving three measurements unavailable. The Reproducer declines to redraw only the failed chain, which would select a repetition based on its outcome. Effective sample size per second also varies with host load and concurrency.

The raw attempt also runs fewer than 100 rounds in part of its sweep. These records illustrate runtime constraints, numerical edge cases, and deviations from the prescribed measurement procedure.

Remaining failures

Failures shared by both handoffs mainly reflect paper–code setup mismatches, typographical errors in the paper, or experimental variability placing measurements outside the compatibility interval. Refinement can also preserve or introduce incorrect experimental choices.

The 84 numerical reversals span several possible mechanisms: stochastic training, timing conditions, changed execution choices, and unresolved paper–code disagreements. Construction may also introduce an incorrect setting, metric interpretation, or repair that later agents inherit. Diagnosing a particular reversal requires tracing its executed configuration and measurement procedure.
